## Enum Values

- **Left**: Panel is positioned on the left side
- **Right**: Panel is positioned on the right side
- **Top**: Panel is positioned at the top
- **Bottom**: Panel is positioned at the bottom
- **Center**: Panel is positioned in the center (for tabs in the main editor area)
